## Reproduction and evidence

Reproducible, non-secret lab scripts are maintained in `scripts/` under this audit directory. Evidence files are in `evidence/`. New AlmaLinux raw installer logs remain under the ignored local path `packaging/build/validation/rc5-r42/` with mode `0600`; hashes and sanitized results are recorded. Existing RC42 logs remain ignored and are labeled “prior RC subset” unless their specific scenario is captured in tracked evidence. A test whose raw evidence is unavailable in the audit directory does not pass the full scenario.
